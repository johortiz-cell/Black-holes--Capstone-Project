What is a black hole?

A black hole is a region of space where gravity is so strong that, once something passes the event horizon, it cannot escape.

🌟

How They Form

Some black holes form when massive stars run out of fuel and collapse under their own gravity.

🌀

Event Horizon

The event horizon is the boundary around a black hole. Crossing it means there is no known way back out.

⏳

Gravity & Time

Very strong gravity affects how time and light behave. This is one reason black holes are so fascinating to study.

🔭

Types

Scientists study stellar-mass, intermediate-mass, and supermassive black holes, among other possibilities.

💫

Accretion Disks

Gas and dust can orbit a black hole in a hot, bright disk before falling inward.

🌌

Galaxies

Supermassive black holes are found at the centers of many galaxies, including our Milky Way.
